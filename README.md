# Fake Skills

Fake Skills is a RuneLite plugin that adds six completely unnecessary skills to Old School RuneScape.

They don't make you stronger.  
They don't unlock anything.  
They don't help you progress.

But the numbers go up, and that's what matters.

## Fake Skills

### 💰 Bank Standing
Gain XP by standing near a bank.

### 👞 Traveling
Gain XP while traveling. Bring a follower along for double XP!

### 🗣️ Yapping
Gain XP by chatting.

### 2X Doubling Down
Gain XP by repeating actions.

### 🙏 Praying
Gain XP by using prayers.

### 🗑️ Exp Waste
Gain XP by doing absolutely nothing.

## Levels

Fake Skills use the standard Old School RuneScape XP curve from levels 1–99.

After reaching level 99, the skill remains level 99 until reaching 200,000,000 XP.

At 200,000,000 XP, the skill becomes level 120.

## Progress

All Fake Skill XP is saved between sessions, so your completely unnecessary progress is never wasted.

## Disclaimer

Fake Skills are entirely cosmetic and do not affect your actual Old School RuneScape skills, XP, account progression, or gameplay.
